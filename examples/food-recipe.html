A weekend loaf the colour of late-afternoon light — saffron blooms in warm cream, brown butter does the heavy lifting, and a thin orange glaze cracks like a winter pond.

The Pantry
Plain flour, sifted 220g
Saffron threads, generous pinch 0.4g
Brown butter, just-warm 140g
Cane sugar & orange zest 180g
Eggs, room temperature 3
The Method
i
Bloom the saffron, gently.
Crush the threads, fold into 60 ml of warm cream and let stand. The cream should turn the colour of dusk before you do anything else.
10 min·Off heat
ii
Take the butter to nut-brown.
Melt slow, swirl the pan until the milk solids speckle and the kitchen smells like toffee. Pour off, save the brown bits.
8 min·Med-low
iii
Whisk eggs & orange-sugar.
Rub the zest into the sugar with your fingers first — wakes the oils. Whisk until the ribbon holds a slow figure-eight.
5 min
